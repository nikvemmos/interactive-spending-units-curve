Interactive capital / spending zone curve

The uploaded image behaves like a capital ÷ annual real spending chart: the red upper line is the capital needed to fund spending until death, expressed in “spending units.” Use the controls to move the current point and reshape the boundaries.

Use real return, not nominal return. Example: 5% nominal minus 2.5% inflation ≈ 2.5% real.
Model
effective real return = realReturn × (1 − taxDrag)
required units = annuity PV × withdrawal gross-up + discounted terminal reserve

annuity PV = (1 − (1+r)^−N) / r
withdrawal gross-up = 1 / (1 − withdrawalTax)
upper red curve = required units × (1 + safetyBuffer)
lower yellow curve = min(zone3Floor, upper curve)
